Boxerbook

Open catalog of professional boxing

Boxerbook is a free catalog of professional boxing worldwide: boxers and their records, bouts with the sources they were read from, upcoming cards with start times and broadcasters, results, world titles, publisher rankings (WBC, WBA, IBF, WBO, The Ring, TBRB) and a directory of boxing gyms. Every bout page lists its sources; when two sources disagree, the page shows both.

On November 27, 2026 the catalog held 205,094 bouts since 1845 in 145 countries, read from 31 sources (counted daily).

Pages

  • Where to watch boxing this week: cards, start times and broadcasters by country
  • Boxing schedule: upcoming fight nights
  • Boxing results with their sources
  • Rankings: the sanctioning bodies, The Ring, TBRB and the Boxerbook rating
  • Boxers: records and bout histories
  • Boxing gyms in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Germany, France and Switzerland
  • The open boxing database: what it holds and where the records come from

For developers and AI assistants

  • MCP server: https://mcp.boxerbook.com/mcp (Streamable HTTP, no key). Guides per client: mcp.boxerbook.com
  • ChatGPT: Boxerbook in the ChatGPT plugin directory
  • Claude: Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector, URL https://mcp.boxerbook.com/mcp
  • JSON API: boxerbook.com/developers, OpenAPI 3.1 at /v1/openapi.json
  • Data access and terms: boxerbook.com/data
  • Source of this page and the server metadata: github.com/maxvotek/boxerbook-mcp

Using the data

Boxerbook data is free to read and cite with a link to the page each fact came from (credit «Data from Boxerbook (boxerbook.com)»). Bulk copies and commercial use are by agreement: info@boxerbook.com.
